Privacy Policy — Block Blast Mobile

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Blast Mobile ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you play our mobile game on Android and iOS devices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration and we do not directly collect or store your name, email address, or phone number. Game progress, high scores, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

To keep the game free to play and process optional purchases, the App uses trusted third-party SDKs that may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, and diagnostic/crash data:

  • Google AdMob: Displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms .
  • RevenueCat, Google Play Billing & Apple In-App Purchase: Processes optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" and power-up packs) and restores previous purchases. Learn more at RevenueCat Privacy Policy .

3. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can reset or limit personalized advertising identifiers at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Children's Privacy

Block Blast Mobile is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
